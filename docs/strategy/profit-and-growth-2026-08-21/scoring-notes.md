# Opportunity scoring notes

This is a directional prioritization model, not observed market data.

Each opportunity was scored from 1 (low) to 5 (high) on:

- supply creation: how directly the activity adds current courts, groups, or recurring sessions
- revenue potential: plausible near-term willingness to pay and gross-profit opportunity
- ease of test: ability to validate manually or with the current product surface before a large build

The displayed priority score is the simple mean of those dimensions, rounded to one decimal. Founding venue partners were assigned 5.0 because the model judged all three factors at or near the maximum; the raw component values are preserved in the artifact dataset. Scores should be replaced with observed pilot economics once available.
